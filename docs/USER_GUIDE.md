# 👤 SplitFlow User & Creator Onboarding Guide

Welcome to SplitFlow! This guide walks creators and buyers through using SplitFlow to register digital assets and split payments on Stellar Soroban.

---

## 🔑 Step 1: Connecting Your Wallet

1. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top navigation bar.
2. Select your preferred Stellar wallet provider (**Freighter**, **Albedo**, **xBull**, or **HANA**).
3. Approve the connection request in your wallet pop-up.

---

## 📝 Step 2: Registering a Digital Asset

1. Navigate to the **Dashboard**.
2. Under **Register Asset**, enter your unique **Asset ID** (e.g. `track_royalty_001`).
3. Add contributor Stellar wallet addresses and their respective split percentages.
4. Ensure the total share percentage equals **100.00%** ($10,000\text{ Basis Points}$).
5. Click **Register Asset** and approve the transaction in Freighter.

---

## 💸 Step 3: Distributing Royalty Payments

1. Enter the target **Asset ID** in the Distribution form.
2. Enter the XLM payment amount.
3. Click **Execute Royalty Split**.
4. Approve the transaction in your wallet. Tokens will automatically split and deposit into each contributor's wallet on-chain!

---

## 📊 Step 4: Tracking Activity & Analytics

- **Activity Feed**: View real-time contract events and Stellar Expert explorer links.
- **Analytics**: View graphical revenue distributions, top assets, and split metrics.
